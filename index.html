Giving AI a Persona

Control the perspective, tone, and expertise level.

🤖 Standard Assistant

"The Internet is a global network of interconnected computers using the TCP/IP protocol suite to link devices worldwide..."

5.2 FEW-SHOT PROMPTING

Using Examples

Show, don't just tell. Establish a pattern for the AI to copy.

Zero-Shot (No Examples)
User: Extract the sentiment of this review: "Battery drains fast."
AI: "The customer in this review is expressing a negative sentiment because they are complaining about the battery life."

(Too chatty, hard to use in code)
Few-Shot (With Examples)
EXAMPLES:
Review: "Love the design." -> {"sentiment": "POS"}
Review: "Too expensive." -> {"sentiment": "NEG"}
Review: "It arrived today." -> {"sentiment": "NEU"}
User: Extract the sentiment: "Battery drains fast."
{"sentiment": "NEG"}

5.2 ITERATIVE REFINEMENT

The Refinement Cycle

Don't expect perfection on the first try. Polish it step-by-step.

Step 1: The Initial Draft
Write a short post about starting a new ICT class.
I am starting a new ICT class next week. Join if you want to learn computers. It will be fun.
Step 2: Adding Constraints & Tone
That's boring. Make it exciting, mention the A/L 2027 batch, and add emojis!
🚀 Get ready! The ultimate ICT class for the A/L 2027 batch is launching next week! 💻 Join us and master the digital world! 🔥 Let's go!
Step 3: Final Polish (Formatting)
Good. Now format it with bullet points for 'Date', 'Time', and 'Venue'. Keep the excitement.
🚀 The Ultimate A/L 2027 ICT Launch! 🔥
Get ready to master the digital world with our new batch!

📌 Date: Next Monday
⏰ Time: 3:30 PM
📍 Venue: IMA Campus, Malabe

💻 Don't miss out! Secure your seat today!
